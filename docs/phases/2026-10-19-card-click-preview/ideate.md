# Ideate — card-click-preview

_Entered 2026-10-19._

- **Problem:** only the image opens the preview; clicking elsewhere on the card does nothing.
- **For whom:** anyone browsing extracted photos.
- **Success:** a click anywhere on a card opens that photo's preview; clicking Download only downloads and does not open the pop-up.
- **If we don't:** users click the caption or card edge and think the preview is broken.
- **Scope:** frontend only (`apps/frontend`). Small change, so a lightweight arc. Issue #41.
