# photo-preview

After a PDF is extracted, the gallery shows each photo only at card size, so small details are hard to see. The user wants to click a photo and see it large in a pop-up over the page, with its caption and Download button, step to the next or previous photo, and close it with ×, Esc, or a click outside. Frontend only.
