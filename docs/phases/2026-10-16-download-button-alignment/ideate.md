# Ideate — download-button-alignment

_Entered 2026-10-16._

**Problem:** In the photo grid, each card's Download button comes right after its photo and description. Cards have different heights, so the buttons sit at different heights and do not line up.

**Who:** anyone looking at extracted photos and saving them.

**Success:** all Download buttons line up. Each one is pinned to the bottom of its card, on the left (the user chose this, 2026-10-16). Nothing else about the card changes.

**If we don't:** the grid looks messy, and the buttons are harder to scan.
